## A. Abandoned checkout (2 emails)

**Where:** Marketing → **Automations** → *Create automation* → template
**"Recover abandoned checkout"**. Shopify already fills in the cart items,
the product photos and the "return to checkout" button automatically.

### Email 1 — about 1 hour after they leave

- **Subject:** You left something behind.
- **Preview text:** Your cart is saved and ready when you are.
- **Heading:** Still thinking it over?
- **Body:**
  Your Behind The Greens gear is still in your cart. Everything we make is
  built for the crews who show up before sunrise, and the people who
  appreciate what they do.
  Your cart is saved, so you can pick up right where you left off.
- **Button:** Return To Your Cart
- **Footer line:** Questions about sizing or shipping? Just reply to this email.

### Email 2 — about 24 hours after they leave

- **Subject:** Your Behind The Greens gear is waiting.
- **Preview text:** Size charts and shipping details are on every product page.
- **Heading:** Your cart is still here.
- **Body:**
  Not sure about the fit? Every apparel page has a full size chart in inches
  and centimeters. Shipping costs are shown at checkout before you pay, and
  unworn items can be returned within 30 days.
- **Button:** Complete Your Order
- **Footer line:** Need a hand? Reply and we'll help.

**To turn it on:** set the delays to 1 hour and 24 hours, paste the copy,
preview it, then click **Turn on**. That's your call.

## B. Shopify Email templates (4)

**Where:** Marketing → **Campaigns** → *Create campaign* → **Shopify Email** →
pick a simple template → swap in the text below. Save each one as a
**template** (⋯ menu → *Save as template*) so it's reusable. Do not click
Send until you're ready.

Use your logo at the top, and black/cream colors to match the site.

### 1. Launch

- **Subject:** New from Behind The Greens: [PRODUCT NAME]
- **Preview:** Built for the crew. Available now.
- **Heading:** [PRODUCT NAME] is here.
- **Body:** [One sentence from the product description.] Made for early
  starts, long days and everything after.
- **Image:** product photo or a still from the film it appears in
- **Button:** Shop Now → product link + `?utm_source=email&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=launch-[name]`

### 2. Restock

- **Subject:** Back in stock: [PRODUCT NAME]
- **Preview:** It sold out last time.
- **Heading:** It's back.
- **Body:** [PRODUCT NAME] is back in stock. Grab yours while sizes last.
  *(Only say "sold out last time" if it actually sold out.)*
- **Button:** Get Yours → product link + `?utm_source=email&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=restock-[name]`

### 3. Updates (new film / story)

- **Subject:** New film: [VIDEO TITLE]
- **Preview:** Go behind the greens at [COURSE NAME].
- **Heading:** [VIDEO TITLE]
- **Body:** [Two sentences about the course and crew.] Watch the full video
  on YouTube.
- **Image:** the video thumbnail
- **Button:** Watch The Video → YouTube link
- **Secondary link:** Shop the gear from the film

### 4. Special Release

- **Subject:** Limited release: [PRODUCT NAME]
- **Preview:** [ONLY IF TRUE: quantity or end date]
- **Heading:** [PRODUCT NAME]
- **Body:** [What makes it special — only real facts.] [If limited: "Only
  [NUMBER] made." / "Available until [DATE]." Only if true.]
- **Button:** Shop The Release

**Before any send:** a test email to yourself (Send test), check links on a
phone, and make sure every [BRACKET] is replaced.
